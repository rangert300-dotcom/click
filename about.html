The Arc

How I Got Here

The Foundations

Systems Administration & MSP

Started in managed service provider environments — the kind of work where you learn every system because you're the only one on call. Built networks from scratch, scripted rollouts, migrated email and files to M365 and Azure VDI. Learned that the best infrastructure is the kind nobody notices until it's gone.

The Scale

Enterprise & Specialized Operations

Took on engagements that tested everything. A 110-person law firm: migrated legacy Active Directory to Entra ID, rolled out Intune and Autopilot, replaced Worldox with NetDocs — all while 80 attorneys kept billing hours. Viking Mississippi cruise line: sole IT authority for a 386-passenger vessel, managing VMware, network, CCTV, IPTV, and Starlink with zero escalation path. When the ship sails, you are the escalation.

The Pivot

Independent Practice

Realized I'd rather be the person businesses call directly than the person behind an MSP brand. Launched Range IT for commercial clients and Telly Tech Services for residential — same diagnostic rigor, two different registers. Started adding web development to the stack via AI-assisted tools, because I learn by doing and the tools got good enough to match my standards.

Current

Fractional Technical Strategist

Now I serve small businesses, nonprofits, and enterprises as a fractional CTO and technical strategist. I build infrastructure, integrate AI tools into real workflows, and translate between technical and business stakeholders. Based in New Orleans, working with clients wherever the work takes me.

Philosophy

The Operator

Three principles that govern every engagement.

Empathy Is Infrastructure

The panicked partner calling at 9 PM is as critical as the server uptime metric. Technical problems are always people problems first. I build systems that respect the humans who use them, and I respond to urgency with clarity, not condescension.

Documentation Is Sanity

If it isn't written down, it doesn't exist. Every system I build comes with runbooks, diagrams, and plain-English documentation. Not because compliance requires it — because the next person who touches this system deserves to understand it. That next person might be you. It might be me in six months.

I Am the Escalation Point

No tiers. No ticket queues. No "let me check with my team." When you work with me, you get me — the person who built the system, knows the system, and will fix the system. Ownership isn't a buzzword. It's what happens when there's nobody else to pass the problem to.

The Differentiator

The Bridge

Most technical people can't explain what they do to a non-technical audience. Most business leaders can't articulate what they need from technology. I live in both worlds.

I've sat in boardrooms translating architecture diagrams into budget justifications. I've sat in server rooms translating business requirements into network topology. The translation isn't just linguistic — it's empathetic. I understand what the CFO is actually worried about, and I understand what the sysadmin is actually dealing with.

This is why my clients don't just hire me for technical work. They hire me because when I'm in the room, decisions get made. Everyone leaves aligned — not because I forced agreement, but because I made the problem legible to everyone at the table.
